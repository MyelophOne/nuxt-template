# Corporate / company overview

Read for a multi-intent business website, not a single-offer campaign. Preserve explicit brand/layout choices.

Default relationship: clear company/offer orientation → useful routes to offerings and work → relevant evidence/differentiation → practical company/process information → decision support/contact. Adapt to the actual audience; do not mechanically copy this order or publish empty company pages to fill navigation.

Give the most important visitor task the strongest entry. Secondary audiences need visible, subordinate routes rather than competing primary CTAs. Relevant service/work collections may use cards; a long company narrative may use an open editorial layout. A short summary can remain sticky beside long detail through the existing composition.

A useful desktop starting point is a strong lead scene followed by mixed-scale offering/work groups and quieter practical information. Mobile prioritizes the lead task/routes before secondary context, retaining evidence and working contact. Large hero imagery stays full-bleed; stock business photography is not mandatory.

Use existing Grid/UI/media/menu/ready-page contracts, actual locale/domain metadata and shared chrome. Root corporate depth/SEO targets apply. No invented history, staff, offices, certifications or customers; missing essential facts require input.

Verify that different legitimate visitors can choose a relevant path, understand the business and reach real content/action without placeholder destinations. Every distinct page template gets the shared visual and dual-output checks.
