# Editorial / article / documentation

Read for sustained reading or editorial browsing; do not impose a sales funnel.

- **Article/guide:** descriptive title/context → readable lead → meaningful body sections with relevant examples/figures → sources/related next reading when useful. Dates/authors must be real.
- **Documentation:** navigable topics/current location and readable explanation/examples; a contents region helps sufficiently long material.
- **Collection:** a prominent lead story with compact secondary items and meaningful categories, or a stable list when browsing/comparison is the main task.

A reading column may have a quieter contents/related region. Use comfortable prose measure and deliberate headings/lists/captions/links, not a card around every paragraph. Large hero imagery follows full-bleed rules, but not every article needs an image. No arbitrary dates, decorative counters or mandatory oversized quotations.

Existing sticky compositions can retain TOC/summary beside long reading; mobile returns to normal flow/disclosure without hiding the article. Keep real section IDs and the shared no-hash/focus contract. Code/tables may scroll locally while prose reflows.

Use the installed blog/ready-page/SEO contract where appropriate; no invented routes or duplicate schema. Root article depth applies without filler. Keep visible content/metadata in SSR with actual locales.

Verify reading order/measure, long headings/URLs/code, table semantics, focus/links, mobile crops and both outputs. Printing is separate scope only when requested.
