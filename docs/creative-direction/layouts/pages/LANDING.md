# Campaign / offer landing

Read for a public single-offer campaign with open structure. Explicit composition wins. A campaign concentrates on one audience/action; a corporate homepage belongs to its own profile. Do not force this funnel onto apps or catalogues.

| Situation | Recommended relationship | Mobile change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Readily understood offer | Concise proposition/action, early concrete example, useful details and decision support | Keep action immediate and example readable |
| Unfamiliar/high-consideration offer | Context, demonstrated solution, scope/process, real evidence, questions, next step | Short leads plus readable depth; no forced pinned story |
| Known audience/high intent | Direct task with requirements, compact proof and relevant support | Put the task before secondary explanation; root minima still apply unless user requests brevity |
| Alternative/selection intent | Fair criteria, meaningful differences and fit before the decision | Retain comparable labels; a real table may scroll locally |

These are relationships, not mandatory blocks/order. A default journey is orientation → demonstration → offering/details → process/fit → decision support → next action, adding useful sections for the root minima. Place evidence beside its claim and answer the actual objection at the decision. Match known traffic context truthfully; never promise conversion improvement from layout alone.

No automatic 6–12 FAQs, three-step process, logo strip, trial or guarantee. Missing proof does not justify invented metrics/customers or realistic-looking fake facts. Root SEO/depth requirements prevail; indexing follows actual goals/configuration, not a blanket rule for every campaign.

Use installed Grid, content compositions, UiCard/Heading/Button and ready-page/SEO contracts after inspection. Large hero media remains full-bleed; an image is not mandatory. Existing reveals/hover remain restrained.

Verify offer/action comprehension, relevant evidence, useful depth, authored rhythm, mobile hierarchy and an honest working result in both themes/outputs.
