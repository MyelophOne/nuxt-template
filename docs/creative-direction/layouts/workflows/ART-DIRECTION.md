# Choose and sustain an authored visual idea

Read for a new public-site identity, an open creative brief or an authorized replacement visual direction. This complements the matched page profile; it is not another page template. Preserve explicit layouts/brand decisions. Ordinary refinements, backend work and established operational screens skip it.

## Make the concept concrete

If the user selects a named aesthetic, read only its matching [style preset](../../presets/INDEX.md) and adapt it to the subject. Do not swap the same composition between preset names. In the early slice, compare the opening with one lower section: identity must survive a shift of scale/density, not depend on a spectacular hero followed by generic filler. A memorable relationship is more useful than a quota of unexpected decorations.

Write one sentence joining the actual subject, intended feeling and visual mechanism: what leads, why that form belongs here and what the visitor should do. Words such as premium, modern or creative alone are not a direction. Name the focal artifact/media, type relationship and rhythm using the existing brief and actual materials. Keep this in the compact site DESIGN.md, not additional mandatory mood/voice/token files.

Explore cheaply before polishing: compare two materially different credible directions at comparable fidelity, within the same facts, palette/font constraints and package capabilities. For visual exploration vary hierarchy, crop, density or narrative order; changing only hue/radius is not a new concept. For an unresolved UX decision vary the user's sequence, entry or commitment point while holding visual quality comparable. Do not confuse those two experiments. Pick against the audience/task and record the reason; do not build several complete sites, invent a weak alternative or spawn agents by default. Explicitly requested variants have their own agreed scope.

## Carry the idea beyond the hero

A signature is a reusable visual relationship, not a gadget. An image treatment, type/material contrast, framing device or rhythm can connect the opening to a later work/detail/action moment. Repeat the underlying logic with restraint, not the same graphic everywhere. Use actual relevant media at useful scale and package Grid/UI compositions; never compensate for weak imagery with a crude substitute or for weak layout with another engine.

Balance visual mass deliberately: large imagery, dense text and strong fills need a coherent counterweight, not necessarily equal columns. Alternate concentrated and quieter passages where meaning changes; avoid accidental cliffs between overcrowding and empty screens. Empty space must connect the subject to its explanation/action rather than isolate unrelated objects. Asymmetry earns its place through emphasis and reading order, not random offsets.

The opening can be the strongest expressive moment while lower content sustains its quality. A work detail, process or contact still needs deliberate type, crops and useful content. Never deliver a spectacular hero over generic filler sections. Quiet or image-led designs can be highly distinctive; no mandatory brutalism, glass, oversized headings, gradients everywhere or fixed style dial.

## Keep creativity usable

Retain familiar navigation, visible actions and readable text. Simplicity reduces uncertainty, not necessarily the number of visible elements: don't conceal needed context to make a screenshot sparse. Tools/docs may express identity through clarity and precise details rather than a marketing spectacle. New behavior needs a user purpose; visual ambition never licenses a new primitive, custom cursor, scroll engine or unrequested 3D.

Inspect one wide and one narrow static slice early. Apply the shared tests: subject swap, silhouette, actual task, media quality and mobile authorship. Check that decorative weight does not outrank the offer/action, the motif survives without motion, and the smallest useful view remains recognizable. Judge rendered evidence, not an invented score, award promise or screenshot similarity metric. Refine the weakest material decision before adding more effects. Keep root content depth, system fonts, replaceable palettes, accessibility and both output modes intact.
