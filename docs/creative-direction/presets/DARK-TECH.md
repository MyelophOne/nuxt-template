# Dark Tech / Minimal Luxe

Trigger: explicit refined dark technology direction. Use actual product/service evidence, not fabricated terminals or metrics. Apply the common contract in INDEX.md.

## Visual grammar

- Build dark-first depth from distinguishable opaque surfaces and readable ink. `#08090A` is an optional ground example, not a required token. Use a controlled brand accent and contrasting secondary text. A visible gradient may connect related tones without white haze or a glowing blob.
- Lead with the offer and readable real product detail or a subject-specific artifact. Give evidence enough space; no fake terminal or 3D mockup by default. Large hero images follow the full-bleed rule.
- Use precise inherited sans type and deliberate weight/size hierarchy. Technical prose does not make all text monospace. Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans require explicit request or established branding.
- Separate panels with surface contrast and subtle dividers; `white/10` is a possible dark divider, not an accessible focus/input-border specification. Required boundaries and focus must be independently visible.
- Keep media crisp. Backdrop blur is optional local separation with a solid fallback; **blur does not create light**. No full-screen filters, laser outlines, permanently glowing buttons or glass on every card.
- Use supported hover/focus changes in tone, border or bounded crop. Short transitions may use `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`; tune to interaction, honor reduced motion and reuse existing reveal timing.
- Design the light theme as a coherent clear-surface counterpart rather than inverting every pixel.

## Prompt example

> Use Dark Tech / Minimal Luxe: deep dark surfaces, legible light text, a subtle brand accent, subtle borders, and real product details. No neon or heavy glassmorphing.

## Check

Inspect supporting text, button labels and panel boundaries at real brightness in both themes. Offer and action must stay obvious with effects disabled; no delayed entry or indistinguishable dark cards.
