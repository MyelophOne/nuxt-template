# Cinematic Portfolio

Trigger: explicit image-led/cinematic portfolio or place presentation. Requires relevant finished media; missing actual work is a brief question, not permission to invent projects. Apply the common contract in INDEX.md.

## Visual grammar

- Give the opening image full width, a purposeful focal point and responsive crop. Full width does not require 100vh: preserve visible orientation/action and short-screen geometry. Prefer a sharp static first frame; no default slideshow, autoplay film or staging preloader.
- Keep concise orientation/captions readable beside or over media; overlays stay local. Do not obscure the subject with giant copy, white veils or fake browser bezels.
- Continue beyond the hero through overview, detail crop, useful explanation and next work/action. Mix panoramic and intimate scales when the subject supports them, not identical alternating rows.
- Combine open layouts and purposeful work cards with bounded crop hover/focus. Useful metadata beats ornamental numbers or invented awards. Retain short context beside longer details through existing desktop sticky APIs when appropriate.
- Derive accent/surface relationships from approved branding or imagery. No mandatory cream/gold luxury treatment, translucent chrome or movement in every frame.
- Pace through content and spacing; cinematic is not permission for scroll hijacking, pinned-screen sequences, parallax engines, horizontal scroll traps or unsolicited 3D.

## Prompt example

> Use Cinematic Portfolio: a full-width main image, expressive shots and details of the work, calm typography, and a well-thought-out project sequence. No sliders, preloaders, or heavy animations.

## Check

Inspect image sharpness, actual responsive dimensions, crop, text contrast and first render. Narrow/short-landscape views must keep the subject and action useful. Verify image feedback and its non-pointer equivalent.
