# Editorial Brutalism

Trigger: explicit editorial/brutalist direction. Useful for reading-led media and expressive portfolios; not a mandate for dense operational workflows. Apply the common contract in INDEX.md.

## Visual grammar

- Lead with a strong headline or real work. Contrast display type with comfortably readable prose and meaningful metadata. Georgia display plus inherited system sans is an available pairing; one system family with distinct weights also works. Custom families require explicit instruction.
- Use Grid alignment anchors, unequal editorial columns and restrained offsets where they clarify the story. Pair a dominant work with quieter supporting content. Recompose to normal reading order on mobile; do not break the package grid to look asymmetric.
- Prefer sharp editorial frames, often radius 0 on owned content surfaces, with thin separators (1px is a starting point). Dividers use semantic theme colors, not black in both modes. Do not globally reset every package control/avatar to square.
- Type, image scale and rules provide depth. Avoid glass, floating pills and shadows on every item. Preserve meaningful image hover/focus and clear control states through supported APIs.
- One deliberate accent or monochrome can work. Use clear white/ink or dark/clear-ink relationships without cream paper or gray fog. Prose measure and supporting-text contrast remain essential.

## Prompt example

> Use Editorial Brutalism: editorial typography, thin dividers, asymmetry on a grid, predominantly right angles, and large, real-life artwork. Fonts are system-specific; palette is from the brief.

## Check

It should read like an edited publication, not a giant title above a spreadsheet. Check Cyrillic wraps, divider contrast, focus, long-form comfort and mobile reading order. Asymmetry must not become misalignment or clipping.
